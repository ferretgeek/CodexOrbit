# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our standard

We welcome constructive participation regardless of experience, identity,
background, or preferred language. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than
the person, assume good intent while still addressing impact, and make room for
others to contribute.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, publishing another person's private information, deliberate
disruption, and repeated personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this standard. They may issue a warning or restrict
participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-reporting
channel when possible. Do not use a public issue if the report contains private
or safety-sensitive information.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
